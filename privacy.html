Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Tuition Tracker ("the app") helps tuition teachers record attendance and homework, and lets parents see their child's attendance and homework. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address, password (stored only in encrypted form by our login provider), name, phone number and whether you are a teacher or a parent.
  • Student details: the student's name, entered by the parent.
  • Class details: subjects, timings, tuition days and the class code created by a teacher.
  • Attendance: present or absent for each tuition day, marked by the teacher.
  • Homework: text notes and photos uploaded by the teacher.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to connect a parent to the teacher's class, to show attendance and homework, and to let a teacher and a parent call each other. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who can see your information

  • A teacher can see the students, parent names and parent phone numbers of the classes the teacher created.
  • A parent can see the teacher's name and phone number, and the attendance and homework of the classes the parent joined.
  • Nobody else can see the information inside the app. Access is limited by database security rules.

Children's information

The app is not for children to use. Student information is entered only by a parent or guardian, who creates the account and agrees to this policy for their child.

Where information is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in the Mumbai (India) region. The website is hosted on Render. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and a script library is loaded from jsDelivr, so those services may receive your device's IP address when the app loads.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Open the app, go to Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the information linked to it. Photos may take a short time to be removed from storage.

Your choices

You can change your phone number and language in Settings, and delete your account at any time. To ask about or correct your information, write to us at the address below.
